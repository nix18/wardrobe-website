## Improvements

- Model generation, photo preprocessing, side and back views, and try-on now use soft, even lighting, similar to a bright overcast day or a large frontal softbox with fill. The prompts aim for natural skin tones and smoother facial transitions with a clothing-catalog look.
- Try-on keeps the softer lighting even when the model reference image has harder light.

Regenerate model images to use the new lighting in future try-ons. Custom templates remain active. Restore defaults under AI Services › Prompt templates to use the new lighting prompts.
