## Improvements

- Front and side try-ons can reuse the original model’s face directly on the phone, preserving facial identity across outfit changes without an extra image request.
- When a pose is selected, the side and back views use the front result as a reference, retaining the same identity.
- With head turns, hats, or accessories that make reuse unsuitable, the app follows the face refinement setting and evens out skin tones.
