## New

- An outfit photo can provide several garment types. Automatic recognition selects each relevant category, which can be adjusted manually. The same photo appears in the corresponding try-on sections, where each selection applies only to its garment type.
- Try-on uses the crop for the selected item. History records the chosen types so they can be restored when trying on the outfit again.

## Fixes

- Combined views now describe the pose separately for each angle and use the correct hand or leg in the back view.
- New photo-based poses retain the source photo as a reference to preserve bent knees, gestures, and perspective. This works with separate or combined views without adding image requests.
- Automatic descriptions for older poses describe visible joints and prioritize your notes. They no longer infer activities like sitting from shortened perspective alone.
- Source photos are included in backup and sync, and mirroring applies to both the photo and skeleton. To restore photographic details for an old template without its photo, recreate it from the original image.
