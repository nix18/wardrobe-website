## Improvements

- The AI-generated badge is a frosted glass capsule at the bottom right of the image. It adapts to the background, and on Android 13 or later its edges have highlights and refraction.
- The badge is anchored to the image in both preview and full-screen mode, so it follows the image when zooming or dragging. The smaller design keeps the text height at least 5% of the image’s shorter side.
- Saved and shared images use the same badge layout.

The glass effect uses [Backdrop (AndroidLiquidGlass)](https://github.com/Kyant0/AndroidLiquidGlass) under Apache 2.0, listed under About › Open source licenses.
