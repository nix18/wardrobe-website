# v1.0.16

- Wardrobe follows your phone’s language and supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, French, Portuguese, Spanish, German, and Japanese. Other languages fall back to English.
- The interface, settings, privacy policy, backgrounds, and pose descriptions follow the app language. On Android 13 or later, you can choose a different language for Wardrobe in system settings.
- The website selects a language by region and remembers your manual preference. Both READMEs have updated English and Chinese screenshots.
