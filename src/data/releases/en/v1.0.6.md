## Improvements

- On-device processing evens out yellow-gray patches and shadows around the chin and mouth in generated model and try-on images. It preserves blush, facial features, and natural shadows on the neck. It runs even when face refinement is off and skips photos supplied directly by the user.
- Face refinement includes the jaw and chin and blends using skin tone, so hair and background colors do not skew the result.
- The default lighting prompt places the light at face level and asks for an even skin tone across the face and neck. Custom templates remain available.

Regenerate a model or try-on to compare the results. If you use customized prompts, refer to the new default lighting language.
