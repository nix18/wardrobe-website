# Wardrobe privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026\
Operator: Yujia Kang (independent developer)\
Contact: <span data-mail="kyj20167|gmail.com">kyj20167 [at] gmail.com</span>

Wardrobe manages clothes on your device and uses AI to tag photos and generate try-on images. The app has no backend of its own, and the developer does not collect, receive, or store your personal information. This policy describes what the app handles, where data goes, and how you can manage it.

## 1. Information stored on your device

- Garments: photos you take or import, names, categories, styles, tags, brands, sizes, prices, and notes.
- Models: names, heights, weights, measurements, photos you provide, and AI-generated model images.
- Try-on history: generated images and the garments, models, poses, and backgrounds used.
- Pose and background templates: source photos, empty scenes, photos used for extraction, and names you supply. Delete them from the template preview.
- AI services: endpoint addresses, model names, and API keys. Android Keystore encrypts the keys, which are excluded from sync and backups.
- Cloud sync: WebDAV or FTP addresses and account details, with encrypted passwords, or your Google account authorization status.

The app stores this information on your device. Uninstalling it deletes all local data.

## 2. When information leaves your device

- AI services: When you tag garments, interpret a query, check duplicates, extract a background, or generate a model or try-on image, relevant photos, text, and measurements go to the AI provider you configured. Its privacy policy governs processing. The developer cannot access this data. Use photos of yourself or obtain permission from the people shown.
- Cloud sync: If you enable it, garment records, models, try-on history, and photos upload to your configured WebDAV or FTP server or to the app-specific folder in your Google Drive.

## 3. Third-party components

- Google Play Services authorization lets the app access its Google Drive folder when you choose Google Drive sync and sign in. Google’s privacy policy applies.
- Google ML Kit detects poses on your device without uploading your photos. Source photos stay on the device. When you use a pose for try-on, its reference photo goes to the AI provider you configured. If you enable sync, it also goes to your configured storage. ML Kit may send performance and usage statistics to Google that exclude photo content.

The app has no advertising, analytics, or push notification components.

## 4. Permissions

- Network access connects to the AI and sync services you configure.
- The system camera and photo picker handle capturing and selecting photos. The app does not request camera or storage permissions. Saving try-on images uses the system media library without additional permissions.

## 5. Photos of people and AI-generated content

- Faces and other biometric information are sensitive personal information. The app asks for separate consent before you first use a photo of a person to generate a model or try-on image.
- Generated images carry an AI-generated label in the app. Saved or shared images include a visible label and embedded metadata. Please keep these labels intact and do not use generated images to impersonate or mislead others.

## 6. Your rights

- View and edit your information directly in the app.
- Delete garments, models, and try-on history from the app. Uninstalling deletes all local data. Delete cloud copies through the relevant storage service.
- Withdraw consent under Profile › About Wardrobe. The app closes after withdrawal and requires consent the next time you open it.

## 7. Minors

Wardrobe is intended for adults. Children under 14 should use it only with a guardian’s consent and guidance.

## 8. Policy changes

If this policy changes, the app will notify you when you next open it and ask for consent again.
