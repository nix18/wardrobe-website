Your data, your device

Your wardrobe, your data

Wardrobe has no app backend. Clothes, photos, and try-on history stay on your phone. To use multiple devices, sync to your own cloud storage.

Local first

All data stays on your device by default. Uninstalling removes the local data. You can export a ZIP backup at any time.

Bring your own AI

Enter your own API key. It is encrypted on your phone and excluded from sync and backups. Photos go to your chosen provider only when you use AI.

Sync across devices

Use Google Drive, WebDAV (Nutstore, Nextcloud, or Synology), or FTP/FTPS. Changes sync in the background. Sign in to the same account on a new phone.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Wardrobe free?

The app is free. Your AI provider bills recognition and image generation by usage and model. The try-on screen estimates the number of image generation calls.

What do I need to get started?

Use an Android 8.0 or later phone and a chat model that accepts images for garment recognition. For AI try-on, add an image generation provider such as Doubao Seedream or Qwen Image.

Are my photos uploaded?

Wardrobe has no app backend. When you use AI, photos go to the provider you configured. If you enable cloud sync, data goes to your chosen Google Drive, WebDAV, or FTP account.

Is my API key secure?

Your API key is encrypted on your phone with Android Keystore and excluded from sync and backups. Re-enter it under AI Services on a new phone.

Is there an iPhone version?

Wardrobe is currently available for Android.
