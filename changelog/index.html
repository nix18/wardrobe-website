v1.0.15-r1

November 6, 2026

Unified item categories and search, show each selected garment in try-on history, and improved headband search and accessory consistency across views.

v1.0.15 revision 1

  • Categories now belong to each piece. The app derives the photo’s categories from its pieces and adds headbands, clips, hair ties, and socks to the available categories. You can filter using the parent Accessories category.
  • Search indexes each piece’s title, description, category, and attributes separately. It recognizes aliases for headbands and displays a crop of the matching piece with a link to its details.
  • Keyboard search preserves your original text. AI-suggested filters are optional, and local text search remains available while vectors are unavailable. Editing a piece updates its search index.
  • Try-on history records the actual pieces selected, including their names, crops, and layer order. Piece details start a try-on for that piece, while outfit details offer a whole-outfit action.
  • Accessories retain independent positions, quantities, sides, and coverage. Front, side, and back views share a reference model and keep hair and socks consistent without adding a separate image repair pass.
  • Existing wardrobes migrate automatically. Photos referenced by try-on history are retained after their source garments are removed.

The app displays 1.0.15. This revision has its own release tag, v1.0.15-r1, and a higher Android version code. It keeps the original download available. You can rerun recognition or edit pieces to add missing details. The upgrade does not trigger paid recognition.

v1.0.15

November 5, 2026

Identify layered garments individually, review their crops and order, and preserve left/right placement and garment construction in back views.

  • Recognition now keeps separate pieces of the same type in one photo, including layers, socks, and bags.
  • Wardrobe distinguishes individual garments from outfits. In the garment list you can edit each piece’s name, area, styling, source photo, and crop, or add, remove, disable, and reorder layers.
  • Try-on uses individually selected crops and allows layers in a shared area to be reordered. History preserves the exact selections and order and asks you to reselect missing pieces.
  • Layers from the same photo use a shared source when possible, preserving real overlap. The model receives piece boundaries and visible construction and preserves asymmetric details, skirt layers, trims, sock and boot height, and the intended way garments are worn.
  • Photo poses now provide locally calculated back-view geometry that preserves left and right joints without a second mirror. Older two-dimensional poses can also provide a back-view reference.
  • Automatic tagging preserves manually corrected items. Text and semantic search include each piece’s name and attributes. Existing garments and try-on history migrate automatically.

To recover previously missed pieces, recognize the photo again or add them manually. The app crops what is visible in the source rather than generating separate product images. Back and depth details that a front photo cannot show are inferred by the model.

v1.0.14

November 5, 2026

Choose a built-in studio or extract a background from a photo. Generate outfit, pose, and background together across three views.

New

  • Choose one of four built-in try-on backgrounds: warm ivory studio, stone wall, cream arch, or sunlit window. You can also extract an empty scene from a photo or save an existing empty scene directly.
  • Backgrounds can be previewed, renamed, deleted, backed up, and synced. Extraction runs once per template, supports queueing, cancellation, and retry, and is reused in future try-ons.
  • Try-on history records the selected background and restores it when reusing an outfit.

Improvements

  • Outfit, pose, and background are generated together. Across views, the camera and lighting stay fixed while the model turns. The app shares reference capacity across garments, builds collages when needed, and explains when there is not enough capacity for a pose or background. Background panels keep equal widths and original pixels to prevent cropping or stretching the scene.
  • Site screenshots, background descriptions, and privacy information were refreshed. Mobile, desktop, and dark layouts were checked. CI avoids duplicate PR builds and deploys the website after a push.

Fixes

  • Startup image cleanup retains all referenced photos. Back-view pose references use explicit body sides and occlusion rules.
  • Old search results can no longer replace newer results after canceling, clearing, or editing a query. Late remote vectors no longer overwrite edits or revive deleted entries.
  • Retouching prompts respect the selected background, and concurrent request logs use safe timestamp formatting.

Background and pose photos reach only your configured provider when you use an associated AI feature. Viewing a preset or saving an already empty scene uses no AI. For an older pose without a source photo, recreate it from the original image.

v1.0.12

November 4, 2026

Generate four views of a model together. Added makeup, retouching, and expression options with a soft portrait style by default.

New

  • Generate a head-and-shoulders reference and front, side, and back full-length references together in one image request, then crop them on the device. When a supplied photo is used as the front reference, only the side and back are generated. Side views face left and the people are centered.
  • Model appearance settings cover makeup, retouching, expression, and style. The default soft portrait style uses matte skin, a gentle expression, and warm portrait lighting while retaining garment and background colors. A studio catalog style is available, and individual try-ons can override the model’s settings.

Improvements

  • Each try-on view is created from its matching model reference, with a head-and-shoulders reference for facial detail. This reduces repeated image edits. Portrait lighting keeps natural facial shadows, supported providers can generate at larger sizes, and saved images retain skin and hair texture.
  • All views use consistent styling instructions. Jackets are open and tops untucked by default unless you specify otherwise. Other clothing visible in a garment photo is excluded.
  • Seedream 4.0, 4.5, and 5.0-lite can generate all requested views together at full resolution. Other services can generate the views in one canvas and crop afterward, which uses one request at a lower resolution per view.

Changes

Separate face refinement and side/back model generation options and templates were removed. A model view can still be replaced with a photo or cleared.

v1.0.5

October 14, 2026

Added face refinement and shortened the reference pipeline. Connection errors are now reported as such.

Improvements

  • Face refinement runs by default on front and side try-ons and side model views. The app finds the head on the device, asks the image model to refine a close-up, and aligns and blends the new face into the original image. Hair, clothing, and background are preserved. The original is retained when detection or refinement fails or the angle changes.
  • Each refined view uses one extra image request. The try-on estimate includes it, and the option can be turned off under Views to generate. Assign a separate provider under Task assignments or edit the prompt template.
  • Side and back references omit the head when a pose is absent and hats or accessories do not need it. When reference slots permit, side generation also uses the model’s original front image for identity.

Fixes

  • An unreachable server now reports a connection failure, distinct from a request timeout.
  • HTTP 499 from a gateway is treated as a server interruption. The timeout retry preference controls whether it retries.
  • Request logs record the status, details, and elapsed time for each failed attempt.

Regenerate the side model image or all views and compare the new try-on results.

v1.0.4

October 13, 2026

Cleaned up grid patterns and grain before reusing AI images as references, reducing artifacts from repeated edits.

Improvements

Generated images are cleaned when saved and before reuse as references, including images from older versions. This reduces grid patterns, diagonal lines, and grain that can build up during repeated AI editing.

  • Detect and remove faint periodic grids of 2, 8, and 16 pixels in luminance while retaining color, garment edges, and real patterns.
  • Resize with antialiased Lanczos filtering to avoid turning fine grids into diagonal artifacts.
  • Lightly smooth grain in flat areas while retaining edges and fabric texture.

For the strongest improvement, regenerate the model views and try the outfit again.

v1.0.0

October 10, 2026

First stable release: manage garments, tag photos with AI, search and find duplicates, generate three try-on views, and sync across devices.

Wardrobe’s first stable release brings together local-first clothing management, AI tagging and duplicate checks, and virtual try-on.

Features

  • Manage photos, categories, styles, storage locations, brands, sizes, prices, notes, and favorites. Categories, styles, and locations are customizable.
  • Tag photos with Chat Completions, Responses, or Messages providers. Recognition runs in the background for bulk imports, and failed items can be retried.
  • Combine local vectors, keywords, and natural-language descriptions in search. For duplicate checks, the app retrieves similar items and compares the candidates with a multimodal model.
  • Generate front, side, and back try-on views. Layer up to eight pieces, with the first selection closest to the body. Use one of 11 built-in poses or create one from a photo with on-device joint detection.
  • Choose from six preset models or generate a reference from your own photo. Body measurements can be estimated using Chinese standard sizing.
  • Sync with Google Drive, WebDAV, or FTP/FTPS, and export or import a ZIP backup. API keys stay on your device.
  • Queue, cancel, and retry recognition or generation in Background tasks.

The counts under Profile and the recently added items link to the wardrobe, favorites, try-on history, models, or item details.

Privacy

  • The app asks for consent on first launch and separately before you send a photo of a person to AI. Withdraw consent under About.
  • Generated pictures carry a visible AI-generated label. Exported and shared images also contain an XMP marker under GB 45438-2025.
  • Data stays on your device by default. Photos go to your configured AI provider only when you use the associated AI feature.
